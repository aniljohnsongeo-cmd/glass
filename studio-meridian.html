We start with the site, not the software.

Every commission begins with a week of doing nothing but walking the plot, reading its light, and talking to the people who'll use the building. Software comes later — once we know what we're actually trying to solve.

That order matters. A house designed from a floor plan template will always feel like one. A house designed from the particular way morning light crosses a particular kitchen table won't.

How a project runs

Four stages, roughly a year

01
Site & brief
Two to three weeks on site and with you, working out what the building actually needs to do before anyone draws a wall.
02
Design
Sketches, models, and enough drawings that you can walk through the building in your head before a single brick is laid.
03
Documentation
Construction drawings detailed enough that the contractor's questions are about the site, not about the drawing.
04
Site supervision
Weekly visits through the build, because the drawing is a plan and the site always has the last word.

Nine people, one studio, in Pune

Founded by Arvind Rao and Leela Menon in 2011, Meridian has stayed deliberately small. Every project gets one lead architect from the first site visit to the final walkthrough, not a rotating cast.

We keep a small run of built work each year on purpose — enough to keep the studio's attention on the site, not spread across a dozen half-watched jobs.
